SCRAVORA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the Scravora iOS game (“the app”) uses and why. The app is published by Hakan Gencturk (“we”). Questions: tuhage@gmail.com.

What we collect

Leaderboards. When you finish a contract or a Sector Sweep run, the app submits your result to our global leaderboards, which run on Google Firebase. Each submission contains:

  • an anonymous Firebase account identifier created automatically on your device (no email, phone number or sign-in),
  • the callsign you choose in Fleet › Pilot, shown publicly next to your result,
  • your best time or score for that leaderboard.

Gameplay analytics. To balance difficulty, the app sends gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, for example when a contract starts or ends, how long it was played, hull damage, powers collected and upgrades bought. These events do not include your callsign, your leaderboard identifier, your location, contacts or any text you type. Analytics can be turned off at any time in Settings (the gear on the home screen) › Share gameplay analytics; turning it off stops collection and resets the analytics data stored on the device.

We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not collect the identifier for vendors for analytics, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your progress, ship, equipment, stars, Alloy and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

How information is used and shared

Leaderboard data is used only to show rankings. Analytics data is used only in aggregate to improve the game. Google LLC processes this data on our behalf as a service provider under its Firebase terms; leaderboard data is stored in a Firestore database located in the EU (Frankfurt). We do not sell or rent personal information.

Retention and deletion

Leaderboard entries remain until we reset a leaderboard or you ask us to remove yours. Because the identifier is anonymous, include your callsign and the leaderboard name when contacting us at tuhage@gmail.com. Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting of our Google Analytics property (two months, the Google Analytics default).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has submitted a callsign that identifies them, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
