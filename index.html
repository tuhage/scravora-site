Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?
Progress, ship, stars, Alloy and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

How do I turn off sound, music or analytics?
Tap the gear on the home screen to open Settings.

How do I change my leaderboard callsign?
Open Fleet and tap the pilot icon in the top bar.

Can I remove my leaderboard entry?
Yes. Email us your callsign and the leaderboard name and we will remove it.
